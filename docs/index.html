Seamless Connection

How PC & Phone Sync Works

Task Thing runs a local WebSocket sync server on your PC (port 44751). No cloud accounts, no third‑party servers — your data stays on your local network.

On Your Phone (Android)

  1. Open Task Thing from your app drawer.

  2. Tap the Sync tab at the bottom.

  3. Point your camera at the PC screen QR code — the scanner auto‑detects the pairing payload with no extra framing.

  4. Or tap Manual IP and enter your PC's local IP (e.g. 192.168.1.50) plus port 44751.

  5. The header pill turns green when the live connection is active.

On Your PC (Desktop Widget)

  1. Launch Task Thing on Windows or macOS.

  2. Click the Sync tab in the navigation bar.

  3. A QR code with your PC's IP, port 44751, and secure sync token is generated instantly.

  4. Share the QR code with your phone, or click Copy IP to paste it manually into the mobile app.

  5. The local WebSocket server starts automatically — no installation required beyond the desktop widget.
