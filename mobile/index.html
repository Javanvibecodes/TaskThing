Documentation, pairing guides, and local privacy terms.
help_outline How to Pair & Sync
  1. Same Wi-Fi: Connect phone and PC to the same home/office Wi-Fi.
  2. Phone Hotspot: Turn on Personal Hotspot on phone and connect PC to it. Direct sync works automatically.
  3. Scan Once: In the PC widget, click Preferences (tune) to view the QR code. On this phone, go to Sync, tap Start QR Scanner, and point at the PC screen.
verified_user Privacy Policy (100% Local-First)

Task Thing is built with privacy as the core foundation:

  • Zero Cloud Databases: No tasks, notes, or timestamps are ever sent to or stored on third-party servers.
  • Zero Analytics & Telemetry: No user tracking, cookies, advertising SDKs, or diagnostic telemetry.
  • Peer-to-Peer Encrypted: Communications between phone and PC occur directly on your local network using token-authenticated sockets.
